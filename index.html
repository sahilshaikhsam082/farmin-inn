Our story

A new hotel,
built around your comfort.

Hotel Farming Inn is on Nipaniya Road in Scheme No. 134, Indore, near Star Square. Whether you are in the city for work, a family visit or a wedding, we make your stay easy, clean and comfortable.

Every room is air-conditioned, with free Wi-Fi and attentive service. Downstairs, our family restaurant serves buffet and à la carte meals, and our banquet hall hosts meetings, parties and family functions.
