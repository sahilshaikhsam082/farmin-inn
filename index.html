Our story

A working farm,
and a quiet place to stay.

Farming Inn sits on open farmland on the edge of Indore, India's cleanest city and the food capital of Madhya Pradesh. We built it for people who want a few days of fresh air, home-style food and unhurried mornings, without travelling far to find them.

The vegetables, herbs and grains served in our kitchen come from our own fields and from farmers nearby. Guests are welcome to walk the rows, join the harvest, or just sit on the verandah with a cup of masala chai.

The Kitchen

From our fields
to your plate.

Our kitchen cooks with the seasons. Mornings start with Indori poha and hot jalebi, and lunch is a slow Malwa thali. In the evening there are wood-fired dishes on the lawn. Most of the produce is picked the same day.
